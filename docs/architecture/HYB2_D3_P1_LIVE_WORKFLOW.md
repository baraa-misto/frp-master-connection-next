## Lifecycle and reports

All edits immediately clear displayed results and disable their exports. Account,
workspace revision and server-computed fingerprint govern sealing and export.
Late computation cannot seal after a superseding revision. The UI also ignores
delivered older responses, including non-aborted responses. A genuine unmount
invalidates authority; React's development StrictMode effect probe does not
invalidate a live revision-zero workspace.

PDF and JSON GETs verify current account, signature, expiry and revision, plus the
existing native decision/qualification currency guards where a native calculation
is included. Native-backed Hybrid handles do not outlive that native snapshot.
Exports render immutable records without executing engineering. Rendering reuses
the existing bounded queue, timeout, PDF-size and concurrency policy; a timed-out
renderer retains its slot until the worker ends.

inputs, source-specific/native properties and conditions, actions, outcomes,
governing traces, physical geometry witnesses and essential limitations. The
Technical Audit retains D2/R3 exact records and, for a native run, the complete
