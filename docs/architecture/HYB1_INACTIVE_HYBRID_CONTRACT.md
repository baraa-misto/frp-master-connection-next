## Capture and evidence boundary

Native capture verifies the existing server signer/account and binds the entire original
request conservatively to both geometry and material identities. A separate internal
provenance seal binds the independently resolved original LRFD case digest. This seal
is a review-fixture orchestration contract; it is not a public load normalizer or a new
signed design authority. Native payload bytes and F9 status are preserved exactly.
Full actions, case/source/frame/reference identities and transport audit remain separate
from the native numerical channel. No reusable handle is exported by the renderer.

Caller inputs cannot supply approval or narrow the required-check list. Actual RHM/3D
methods remain unadopted; reference ratios never cover actual mechanisms. Synthetic
coverage exercises completeness only in an explicitly test-only identity and scope.
Even a complete synthetic record cannot activate a public method or qualification.

Unknown ASD combinations or condition/product policies are incomplete. No ASCE factor
is automatically imported. New routines never infer service actions from factored
results, erase eccentric moments, copy P/n into an eccentric arrangement or substitute
column bearing for receiving-W net/block behavior. Both W block identities persist.

doubler, not the actual inclined Angle-to-W assembly. Positive directional magnitudes
and uniform direct demand are explicit. Net/gross path and reinforcement engagement
remain unestablished for transferring the routine to actual geometry. Source SF4,
75 ksi x 0.17 bolt allowable and selected shape column remain reference-specific.

print truncation and storm inconsistency are preserved as separate discrepancy evidence.

See HYB1_RHM_DECISIONS_REQUIRED.md for the six exact source/domain questions and smallest
next owner decision. None constitutes Section 2.3.2 qualification or C13 acceptance.
