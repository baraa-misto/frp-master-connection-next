## Status control

Risk statuses are **Open**, **Monitoring**, **Mitigated**, **Accepted by approval authority**, and **Closed**. All Stage 0.1 risks below are Open. Documentation or geometry support alone does not mitigate an engineering risk. Closure requires the stated resolution and controlled evidence.

## Escalation and closure

- Any risk that can affect applicability or structural conclusions blocks engineering approval for the affected capability.
- Risk acceptance requires a named approval authority, documented rationale, bounded scope, date, and review trigger; a software implementer cannot accept an engineering risk unilaterally.
- New source information, errata, failed verification, material changes, or expanded geometry reopens affected risks.
- Closed risks retain their evidence and history; they are not deleted from the register.

## Stage 0.1 limitation

This register records unresolved engineering work. It does not supply a substitute method, numerical criterion, resistance, capacity, utilization, or calculation result.
