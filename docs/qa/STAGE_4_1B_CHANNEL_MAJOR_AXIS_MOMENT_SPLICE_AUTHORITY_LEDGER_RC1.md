## Channel flange branch authority

Reuse:

`RATIONAL_BALANCED_FLANGE_FACE_COUPLE_DECOMPOSITION_RC1`.

The complete Slice 6 flange wrench is resolved into outer and inner longitudinal force lines at the actual plate centroid positions.

Exact:

`F_o+F_i=N_f`.

`e_oF_o+e_iF_i=m_T,f`.

Both branch force lines remain at:

`T=b_f/2`.

Therefore the physical branches retain the Channel flange transverse eccentricity relative to the exact Channel centroid.

No flange force line is moved toward the web.

## Channel web-face branch authority

New method:

`RATIONAL_CHANNEL_WEB_FACE_SHEAR_CENTER_COUPLE_DECOMPOSITION_RC1`.

The Slice 6 web longitudinal force and local major moment split equally between the two identical web-face plates because those actions are symmetric about the physical Channel web centroid.

The Slice 6 web major shear does **not** split equally.

Let the back/opening plate force-line offsets from the web centroid be:

`e_back<0<e_open`.

Solve:

`Q_back+Q_open=V_w`.

`-e_backQ_back-e_openQ_open=m_L,w`.

This exact statics solution physically represents the Slice 6 web free-torsion/shear-center reference effect.

The branch forces may be unequal and may have opposite signs.

No 50/50 shear rule.

## Generated torsion authority

The centroidal torsion in supported RC1 loading is generated by shifting major shear from the selected Channel shear center to the centroid reference.

It is not user torsion.

The Stage 4.1B web-face force couple closes that reference-generated wrench.

The calculation does not establish:

- Saint-Venant torsional strength;
- warping normal stress;
- bimoment capacity;
- Channel distortion stiffness;
- torsional connection stiffness.

Those remain outside RC1 and require review/qualification.

## Stage 2.5A group-demand authority

Every physical plate branch is translated to the actual beam-side bolt-group reference and resolved using accepted Stage 2.5A.

This is particularly important for the web branches, where:

- vertical shear is unequal;
- free major-axis moment is retained;
- exact group-reference translation can create substantial eccentric group moment.

Simple force-per-bolt division is not authorized when the actual group moment is nonzero.

## Unequal common-bolt plane authority

Reuse:

`RATIONAL_ASYMMETRIC_TWO_PLANE_COMMON_BOLT_SHEAR_RC1`.

Governing physical bolt utilization is the maximum plane utilization.

No equal-plane assumption and no blind double-shear capacity sum.
