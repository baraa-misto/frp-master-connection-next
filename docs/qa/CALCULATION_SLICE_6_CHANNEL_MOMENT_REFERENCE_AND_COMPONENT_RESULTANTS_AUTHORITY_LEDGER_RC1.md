# FRP Master Connection — Calculation Slice 6 — Channel Major-Axis Moment Reference and Component Resultants — Authority Ledger RC1

## Authority purpose

Calculation Slice 6 supplies the Channel-specific demand/reference authority required before Stage 4.1B can implement a physical Channel beam moment splice.

It prevents Stage 4.1B from copying frozen W/I assumptions that are not valid for a singly symmetric Channel.

## Source-derived requirements

ASCE/SEI 74-23 establishes that:

- Channel and other unsymmetrical member/connection eccentricities must be considered;
- connection actions and deformations must be consistent with structural analysis;
- flexural splices must transmit applicable forces and moments;
- shear-carrying parts must retain shear, bolt-group eccentricity effects, and their proportion of moment;
- major-axis Channel member behavior is within the member-design framework;
- open-section torsion and moment-resistant FRP connections require rational analysis and Section 2.3.2 review/qualification.

The standard does not prescribe the complete Calculation Slice 6 Channel region decomposition or rational geometric shear-center formula.

## Exact geometry authority

Calculation Slice 6 reuses the accepted sharp-corner unlipped Channel profile:

- one clear-height web rectangle;
- two equal full-width flange rectangles;
- no fillets;
- no lipping;
- no built-up/back-to-back behavior.

Exact solid-region geometry supplies:

- area;
- centroid;
- major-axis inertia;
- minor-axis inertia;
- zero product of inertia for the supported singly symmetric orientation;
- actual top-flange, web, and bottom-flange centroid coordinates.

The Channel centroid is not replaced by the web mid-plane or the shear center.

## Homogeneous longitudinal-section boundary

The RC1 linear-elastic region integration requires the Channel web and flanges to share the same longitudinal modulus/material architecture.

A hybrid longitudinal section requires transformed-section/rational authority outside RC1.

## Rational component-resultant authority

Method:

`RATIONAL_ELASTIC_CHANNEL_REGION_RESULTANT_DECOMPOSITION_RC1`.

Longitudinal stress:

`σ_L(V)=P_L/A+M_TV/I_T`.

For region `i`:

`N_i=P_LA_i/A+M_TA_iV_i/I_T`.

`m_T,i=M_TI_i,T,c/I_T`.

The complete component wrench is reported at the actual physical region centroid.

No local web/flange moment is discarded.

## Transverse eccentricity authority

Because Channel region centroids do not lie on one web-centered line, each longitudinal region force has an individual transverse minor-axis moment contribution:

`M_V,i=(T_i-T_c)N_i`.

For the supported centroidal axial + major-axis moment state:

`ΣM_V,i=0`.

The individual nonzero terms remain controlling trace records for the later physical Channel splice.

## Shear-center source authority

Exactly one source controls the Channel shear-center coordinate.

### Rational mode

`RATIONAL_THIN_WALL_CHANNEL_SHEAR_CENTER_RC1`.

Median-line geometry:

`h_m=d-t_f`

`b_m=b_f-t_w/2`.

Shear-center offset:

`e_w=3t_fb_m^2/(t_wh_m+6t_fb_m)`.

Absolute coordinate:

`T_sc=t_w/2-e_w`.

This is project rational authority and always requires engineering review.

### Explicit verified mode

`EXPLICIT_VERIFIED_CHANNEL_SHEAR_CENTER_RC1`.

An exact coordinate may be supplied only with controlled provenance.

No silent numerical override or source blending.

## Multi-reference action authority

Calculation Slice 6 interprets:

- axial force through the exact centroid;
- major-axis moment at the exact centroid;
- major shear through the selected shear center.

The equivalent canonical centroid wrench includes:

`M_L,C=(T_c-T_sc)V_V`.

This torsion is generated by reference eccentricity and remains mandatory even though user torsion is zero.

## Web shear / free torsion authority

RC1 assigns 100% of major shear to the web component at the web centroid.

The web component additionally receives:

`m_L,web=(T_w-T_sc)V_V`.

The web force-line torsion and free torsion close exactly to the canonical centroidal torsion.

No torsional resistance or warping stress is calculated.

## Optional rational shear-flow diagnostic

For rational shear-center mode:

`H_f=V_Vt_fh_mb_m^2/(4I_T,m)`.

`H_fh_m=m_L,web`.

This diagnostic records the shear-center derivation and is not yet a physical flange-splice force allocation.

## Exact equilibrium authority

The top-flange, web, and bottom-flange component wrenches must recover exactly:

- axial force;
- major shear;
- major-axis moment;
- generated torsion;
- zero minor shear;
- zero minor-axis moment.

No tolerance residual dumping.

## Major-axis couple diagnostic

The Channel result includes:

- flange lever arm;
- exact antisymmetric flange couple;
- full-moment `M/z` reference;
- residual major moment.

`M/z` remains noncontrolling.

The complete 3D component wrenches are the future Stage 4.1B design handoff.

## Review / disclaimer authority

Always:

`RATIONAL_METHOD_ENGINEERING_REVIEW_REQUIRED=true`.

Mandatory disclaimer ID:

`CHANNEL_MOMENT_REFERENCE_AND_COMPONENT_RESULTANTS_DISCLAIMER_RC1`.

The disclaimer distinguishes:

- exact geometric centroid/properties;
- rational linear-elastic region integration;
- rational or explicit shear-center source;
- generated open-section torsional demand;
- absence of torsional/warping resistance design;
- required structural-analysis consistency;
- required Section 2.3.2 qualification/review for the final Channel moment splice.

## No strength / product authority

Calculation Slice 6 adds no:

- splice plates;
- bolts;
- bolt-group demand;
- torsional resistance;
- Channel connection capacity;
- product selector/workspace;
- connection PASS/FAIL;
- stiffness or rotation classification.

Stage 4.1B remains unimplemented after this slice.
