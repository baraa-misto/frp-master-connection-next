The overall product, production toolchain, and deployment are not globally approved or frozen, and a registered decision does not create calculation support or an approved engineering method. Dates record entry into this register, not a claim of external certification.

Allowed statuses are exactly:

- `Approved`
- `Provisional`
- `Pending Engineering Decision`
- `Pending Architecture Decision`
- `Superseded`
- `Rejected`

An `Approved` decision remains controlling until a recorded successor is approved. It is not reopened without a genuine engineering, safety, security, or architectural conflict supported by evidence. `Provisional` identifies a working recommendation that may guide reversible foundation work but is not an approved production selection. Pending entries record decision scope, impacts, and authority without selecting an outcome. `Superseded` and `Rejected` require a rationale and relationship to the controlling decision.

## Change record rules

- IDs are never reused or renumbered.
- A changed outcome receives a new decision ID and links both `Supersedes or superseded by` fields; the former entry becomes `Superseded` only when the successor is approved.

- Architecture approval is required before a provisional technology or provider becomes a production dependency.
- Security, business, and legal approval remain required where identified, even when the register’s allowed status is `Pending Architecture Decision`.
