# FRP Master Connection — Stage 3.5A-R2 Material-Axis Correction and Three-Component Force Expansion Decision

## Decision

Stage 3.5A-R2 completes two owner-identified issues in the concrete-wall paired clip-angle connection:

1. Correct the Stage 3.5A material-axis visualization so every displayed FRP physical region has the correct region-specific `LW / CW / TT` basis.
2. Expand the shear-connection force input from one vertical reaction component to three signed force components:
   - Major shear;
   - Minor shear;
   - Axial force.

No user-applied moment is introduced.

The product remains under **Shear Connections** and is not a moment-resisting connection.

## Force-frame decision

Stage 3.5A-R2 uses the existing concrete-wall local frame:

- `H_W`: horizontal along the wall;
- `V_W`: vertical upward along the wall;
- `N_W`: wall outward normal toward the connected member.

Normal UI mapping:

- **Major shear** = force along `V_W`;
- **Minor shear** = force along `H_W`;
- **Axial force** = force along `N_W`.

Sign convention:

- positive Major shear: `+V_W`;
- positive Minor shear: `+H_W`;
- positive Axial force: `+N_W`, tension/pull away from the wall;
- negative Axial force: `-N_W`, compression toward the wall.

The complete user force is:

`F = (V_minor, V_major, P_axial)` in `(H_W, V_W, N_W)`.

User-applied free moment remains:

`M_user = (0,0,0)`.

The existing Stage 3.5A-R1 default remains:

- Major shear = `-4 kip`;
- Minor shear = `0 kip`;
- Axial force = `0 kip`.

## Eccentricity-induced transfer moments

All moments generated by translating the three-component force between physical reference points remain mandatory:

`M_to = M_from + (r_from - r_to) × F`.

These moments are transfer/equilibrium moments, not user-applied moment-connection actions.

They remain in:

- branch traces where branch allocation is authorized;
- the combined wall-interface wrench;
- the external anchor-design handoff.

## Pair-symmetry applicability

The existing exact paired-angle half-share equation is not changed.

Stage 3.5A-R2 expands its **applicability authority** only for force components lying in the symmetry plane.

When:

`V_minor = 0`

and all geometric/material symmetry conditions pass, the force vector:

`F_sym = (0, V_major, P_axial)`

is exact-mirror symmetric and may be split equally:

`F_+ = F_- = 0.5 F_sym`.

When:

`V_minor != 0`

the horizontal wall force is antisymmetric with respect to the paired-angle mirror plane and the existing symmetry proof does not authorize a positive/negative branch allocation.

Therefore:

- the complete combined wall wrench remains exact;
- the full anchor layout remains in the external handoff;
- positive/negative branch allocation for the complete action is `NOT_EVALUATED`;
- specialized anchor software must distribute the full combined action to the complete anchor layout.

No 50/50 split is fabricated for nonzero Minor shear.

## Common member-group action

For the common member through-bolt group:

- Major shear `V_W` and Axial force `N_W` lie in the connected-leg/member plane and may enter the accepted in-plane Stage 2.5A demand path where current applicability holds.
- Minor shear `H_W` acts along the common bolt axes and is retained as explicit bolt-axis/normal action.

Stage 3.5A-R2 does not generate bolt tension or prying resistance for the Minor-shear component.

Required limitation for nonzero Minor shear:

`COMMON_MEMBER_GROUP_BOLT_AXIS_RESPONSE = NOT_EVALUATED`.

## Axial wall-side boundary

Axial force normal to the concrete wall may require:

- anchor tension;
- wall/contact compression;
- clip-angle wall-leg bending;
- prying;
- contact/anchor force partition.

These are not calculated internally.

For nonzero Axial force, required limitations include:

- `CLIP_ANGLE_WALL_LEG_AXIAL_TRANSFER_AND_PRYING = NOT_EVALUATED`;
- `WALL_NORMAL_CONTACT_AND_ANCHOR_FORCE_PARTITION = EXTERNAL_DESIGN_REQUIRED`.

The exact full wall wrench remains exported.

## Minor-shear wall-side boundary

For nonzero Minor shear:

`MINOR_SHEAR_PAIRED_BRANCH_ALLOCATION = NOT_EVALUATED`.

The external handoff shall include:

- the full combined wall wrench;
- all positive and negative anchor coordinates as one complete wall-anchor layout;
- group ownership/geometry;
- explicit statement that internal branch allocation is not authorized.

## Material-axis decision

No Stage 3.5A-R2 material property or R14B material basis change is authorized unless a read-only audit first proves the backend-authoritative basis is itself wrong.

The intended outcome is:

- exact backend-authoritative `LW`, `CW`, and `TT`;
- three mutually perpendicular unit directions for every FRP physical region;
- `TT` parallel to the physical through-thickness normal of that region;
- one embedded indicator per physical region under the existing presentation rules.

The correction should normally be visualization/transform/binding only.

## Required material-axis coverage

For every Stage 3.5A-R2 connected profile:

- Flat Plate: plate;
- Angle: both legs;
- Channel: web and both flanges;
- W/I: web and both flanges;
- RHS: all physical walls, no cavity;
- SRS: accepted solid-region basis.

For each paired clip angle:

- connected leg;
- concrete-side/wall leg.

Concrete receives no FRP material axes.

## Historical compatibility

Historical contracts remain:

- `3.5A-RC1`;
- `3.5A-R1-RC1`.

Stage 3.5A-R2 introduces:

`3.5A-R2-RC1`.

Historical requests, engineering results, and fingerprints remain exact.

## Connection qualification boundary

Stage 3.5A's original simple-shear source basis remains the qualified reference case.

Whenever Minor shear or Axial force is nonzero, retain:

`NONMAJOR_FORCE_CONNECTION_QUALIFICATION = NOT_EVALUATED`.

This does not prevent exact force/wrench transfer, existing supported local checks, or external anchor handoff. It prevents Stage 3.5A-R2 from claiming that the complete nonmajor-force connection is prescriptively qualified by the original simple-shear source basis.

## Engineering boundary

No new:

- demand equation;
- resistance equation;
- anchor capacity;
- concrete capacity;
- moment-connection method;
- prying/tension method.

Ordinary whole-connection `PASS` remains prohibited.

Known supported numerical failure retains `FAIL` precedence.
