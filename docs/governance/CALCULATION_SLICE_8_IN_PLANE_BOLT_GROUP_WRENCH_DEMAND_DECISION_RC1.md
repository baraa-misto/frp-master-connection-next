# FRP Master Connection — Calculation Slice 8 — Generalized In-Plane Bolt-Group Wrench Demand — Decision RC1

## Status

Controlled shared calculation authority — RC1.

## Reason for slice

Stage 4.2 pre-implementation audit exposed an accepted-engine applicability gap:

- Stage 2.5A transfers in-plane force and the moment generated by force-line eccentricity;
- Stage 2.5A deliberately does **not** transfer an independently applied in-plane free/group moment;
- therefore an interface wrench `(F_A, F_B, M_C)` with nonzero independent `M_C` cannot be routed through Stage 2.5A without discarding action or inventing an equivalent force location.

No frozen Stage 2.5A behavior shall change.

Calculation Slice 8 adds a successor-safe equal-stiffness elastic bolt-group wrench engine that directly accepts the complete in-plane wrench.

## Method identity

`RATIONAL_ELASTIC_IN_PLANE_BOLT_GROUP_WRENCH_DEMAND_RC1`

## Scope

Input:

- explicit physical bolt coordinates `(A_i,B_i)`;
- explicit wrench reference `(A_R,B_R)`;
- signed in-plane force `(F_A,F_B)`;
- signed independently applied free moment `M_C,R`.

The engine transports the complete wrench to the exact bolt-group centroid and distributes:

1. equal direct force shares; and
2. equal-stiffness elastic moment correction.

It supports:

- concentric force;
- eccentric force;
- force plus independent free moment;
- pure independent moment;
- sign reversal;
- arbitrary nondegenerate bolt patterns.

## Exact mechanics

Exact bolt centroid:

`A_c = (1/n) Σ A_i`

`B_c = (1/n) Σ B_i`

Exact centroidal polar coordinate sum:

`J = Σ[(A_i-A_c)^2 + (B_i-B_c)^2]`

Exact transported in-plane moment:

`M_C,c = M_C,R + (A_R-A_c)F_B - (B_R-B_c)F_A`

Per-bolt equal direct share:

`q_A,d = F_A/n`

`q_B,d = F_B/n`

Per-bolt elastic correction:

`q_A,m,i = -M_C,c(B_i-B_c)/J`

`q_B,m,i = +M_C,c(A_i-A_c)/J`

Total:

`q_A,i = q_A,d + q_A,m,i`

`q_B,i = q_B,d + q_B,m,i`

Exact proof:

`Σ q_A,i = F_A`

`Σ q_B,i = F_B`

`Σ[(A_i-A_c)q_B,i - (B_i-B_c)q_A,i] = M_C,c`.

## Numeric authority

The mechanics/proofs shall use exact rational arithmetic derived losslessly from finite Decimal inputs.

Binary float is prohibited.

Primary proof records retain exact numerator/denominator values.

For interoperability with existing Decimal-based resistance engines, each exact rational force component is projected deterministically to Decimal using:

- precision `80`;
- rounding `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`.

Per-bolt magnitude is:

`sqrt(q_A,i^2 + q_B,i^2)`

projected under the same Decimal-80 context.

Decimal projection is not used as the equilibrium proof.

No epsilon tolerance and no residual redistribution are permitted.

## Relationship to Stage 2.5A

Stage 2.5A remains frozen and authoritative within its accepted scope.

Slice 8 is not a mutation or replacement.

Where:

- `M_C,R = 0`; and
- the existing Stage 2.5A force/reference applicability holds; and
- equal direct bolt shares apply;

Slice 8 shall reproduce the same equal-stiffness eccentric-force mechanics.

The implementation shall add compatibility tests against existing Stage 2.5A fixtures without changing Stage 2.5A.

## Pure moment

Pure independent moment is explicitly supported when `J > 0`.

For:

`F_A = F_B = 0`

and:

`M_C,R != 0`

the bolt vectors form the required self-equilibrating elastic couple.

No artificial force line or equivalent eccentricity is invented.

## Degenerate groups

If `M_C,c != 0` and `J = 0`:

`CALCULATION_NOT_SUPPORTED_ZERO_GROUP_POLAR_SUM`.

A one-bolt or otherwise zero-J group may still transfer in-plane force when `M_C,c = 0`.

- Decimal-80 projected per-bolt vectors;
- Decimal-80 per-bolt magnitudes;
- exact force/moment proof;
- deterministic fingerprint;
- applicability/status trace.

No resistance/capacity calculation.
