# Terminology Register

## Control rules

Terms in this register are canonical through Stage 3.1 documentation. A definition does not imply that a persisted schema, frontend engineering workflow, material catalog, section-property calculation, connector resistance method, or calculation outside an explicitly authorized engine slice exists. Names marked **Provisional** may change through an approved architecture decision; engineering meaning may change only through qualified engineering review and recorded governance.

| Term | Status | Controlled meaning | Distinctions and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beam-to-Concrete Wall — Paired FRP Clip Angles | Approved Stage 3.5A/R1 product term | Separate shear connection with one horizontal FRP connected member, symmetric positive/negative FRP clip angles, one common member group, two mirrored wall-anchor groups, and a finite concrete wall. R1 accepts Flat Plate, Angle, Channel, W/I, RHS, and SRS. | It is not the frozen profile-supported Paired Clip-Angle connection and does not imply evaluated concrete or anchor capacity; historical `3.5A-RC1` remains W/I-only. |
| External anchor-design handoff | Approved Stage 3.5A boundary | Deterministic backend-authored record of wall frame, branch and combined interface wrenches, anchor coordinates, edge distances, diameter, hole, embedment, and exact governing limitations for downstream design. | It is demand/geometry transfer only; it contains no concrete or anchor resistance, utilization, or ordinary PASS. |
| Signed vertical reaction shear | Approved historical Stage 3.5A/R1 action | The sole user-applied historical Stage 3.5A/R1 action, signed on the controlled vertical wall-frame axis. | R2 succeeds this surface with three forces; every contract still prohibits user-applied moments and retains backend-generated eccentric moments. |
| Major / Minor / Axial wall-frame force | Approved Stage 3.5A-R2 action | Three signed force components mapped respectively to `V_W`, `H_W`, and `N_W`; positive axial force pulls away from the wall. | The product remains a shear-category connection. No user moment, Minor paired-branch distribution, bolt tension/prying response, or wall contact partition is implied. |
| Branch-resolved external handoff | Approved Stage 3.5A-R2 mode | Exact positive and negative branch wrenches plus the combined wall wrench, allowed only when Minor shear is zero and pair/action symmetry passes. | Equal sharing applies to the authorized Major/Axial in-plane action only. |
| Combined-layout external handoff | Approved Stage 3.5A-R2 mode | Complete combined wall wrench plus both owner-qualified anchor layouts when Minor shear is nonzero. | Complete branch allocation is `NOT_EVALUATED`; no 50/50 Minor split or branch wrench is fabricated. |
| Direct side-lap Angle/Channel to concrete wall | Approved Stage 3.5B product term | Separate connection in which a finite Channel web or one selected Angle leg overlaps the exterior face behind a finite wall free end and the connected member continues beyond the wall. | It is not the paired clip-angle wall connection. No intermediate connector, concrete capacity, anchor capacity, or ordinary whole-connection PASS is implied. |
| Side-lap length | Approved Stage 3.5B geometry input | Physical overlap length measured behind the wall free-end plane from `L_LAP = 0` toward negative `L_LAP`. | Editing lap length moves the member start only; it never moves the anchor group unless the explicit center action is invoked. |
| Center anchor group in overlap | Approved Stage 3.5B explicit action | Engineer-requested one-time placement of the direct anchor-group centroid at the physical side-lap midpoint. | It is not a persistent auto-centering mode and does not authorize frontend geometry or wrench calculation. |
| Side-lap frame | Approved Stage 3.5B frame | Right-handed backend frame with longitudinal `L_LAP`, in-contact-plane transverse `S_LAP`, and wall-outward normal `N_W`, satisfying `L_LAP x S_LAP = N_W`. | Axial, Major, and Minor map to `L_LAP`, `S_LAP`, and `N_W`; user moments remain zero. |
| Brace/beam connection | Approved Stage 3.5A-R1 presentation term | User-facing group/option wording for an existing shear connection whose physical topology can serve a zero-degree beam as well as a brace. | Presentation only; stable internal connection types, request identities, routes, geometry, and fingerprints do not change. |
| One-anchor wall-group distribution limitation | Approved Stage 3.5A-R1 applicability boundary | A 1×1 wall-anchor group retains its exact branch wrench but exposes no nominal per-anchor force distribution when that single anchor cannot equilibrate the retained branch moment. | The full wrench remains in the external handoff as `EXTERNAL_DESIGN_REQUIRED`; the moment is never discarded and no anchor/concrete capacity or ordinary PASS is inferred. |
| Render-equivalent right-handed box basis | Approved Stage 3.5A-R1 visualization term | Right-handed basis used to display an axis-aligned/reflected backend box with the same center, extents, and physical volume when the serialized reflection basis is left-handed. | It is presentation-only and cannot alter backend geometry, physical path, material basis, engineering fingerprint, or owner identity. |
| ConnectionProject | Approved concept; name provisional | User-facing engineering project concept that groups engineering content for persistence and workflow. | Do not assume it is the persistence row or calculation input object. |
| ProjectRecord | Provisional | Ownership and lifecycle envelope for a saved project, including identity references, timestamps, schema/version references, and storage metadata. | Kept outside the engineering fingerprint except where an engineering value is intentionally included. |
| EngineeringProjectDocument | Provisional | Versioned, JSON-compatible canonical calculation input document held by a `ProjectRecord`. | Contains engineering inputs, not authentication, billing, or UI display state. |
| JointAssembly | Approved concept | Complete central engineering object for a physical joint, including members, interfaces, components, actions, load paths, shared regions, views, and result relationships. | **Provisional — PRV-015:** a project schema may contain one or more assemblies even if the MVP exposes one. |
| MemberInstance | Approved concept; name provisional | A specific connected or supporting member in a `JointAssembly`, with identity, section/material references, orientation, spatial frame, and role. | Distinct from reusable section or material data. |
| ConnectionInterface | Approved concept; name provisional | Explicit physical load-transfer relationship between two participants in the joint. | May be direct member-to-member or member-to-connecting-component. Stage 1.3C2A does not assign it to a surface; that targeting contract remains Stage 1.3C2B. |
| DirectBoltedInterface | Approved concept; name provisional | `ConnectionInterface` in which members are physically bolted directly together. | No artificial connector component is inserted. |
| ConnectingComponent | Provisional | Internal umbrella term for a physical load-transferring component such as a T-section, plate, angle, gusset, or bracket. | Does not include bolts or other fasteners. Its material/qualification remains explicit. |
| Fastener | Provisional | Internal umbrella term for discrete fastening hardware. | Reserved separately from `ConnectingComponent`; a bolt is a kind of fastener. |
| Bolt | Approved concept; name provisional | Fastener with controlled identity, material, diameter designation, master center, holes through penetrated layers, and ordered stack details. | Not modeled as a connecting component. No capacity is implied by definition. |
| BoltGroup | Approved concept; name provisional | Named collection of bolts whose canonical master centers and relationship support an interface, component, shared-region, drawing, and reporting context. | One physical group may participate in more than one result level. |
| BoltStack | Approved concept; name provisional | Ordered physical sequence through the layers penetrated by one bolt, including holes, head, nut, washers, grip, shear planes, and bearing layers. | Preserves alignment, access, and interference information. |
| Doubler | Approved concept | Explicit reinforcement component attached to a host component and face. | Parent and doubler thickness are not automatically additive; structural credit requires a qualified method. |
| SharedRegion | Approved concept; name provisional | Identified region of a supporting member or component that receives demand from more than one interface or load path. | Requires combined checking where applicable. |
| LoadPath | Approved concept; name provisional | Explicit, reviewable route by which actions transfer through interfaces, components, fasteners, shared regions, and supporting members. | The software may verify a declared path but may not silently invent one. |
| MemberEndAction | Approved concept; name provisional | Signed action applied by a member to the joint assembly for one load combination, coordinate frame, and reference point. | Initially treated as a final factored design action; no silent additional factors. |
| ForceReferencePoint | Approved concept; name provisional | Explicit location at which a `MemberEndAction` is defined. | Any offset to a physical interface is preserved. Allowed points by template remain pending. |
| LoadCombination | Approved concept | Named grouping under which member-end actions are entered and results are evaluated. | Treatment of combinations that are not factored remains pending. |
| SpatialFrame | Approved umbrella concept; name provisional | Explicit global or local coordinate-frame concept used to locate geometry or express actions. | Stage 1.3A distinguishes symbolic identity from a numerically resolved Cartesian frame. |
| CoordinateFrameReference | Implemented | Symbolic coordinate-frame identity and provenance with a controlled frame kind and owner where required. | Distinct from `CartesianFrame3D`; it contains no resolved origin or basis. Stage 1.3C1 resolves each placed component directly in global coordinates but creates no assembly-wide frame or placement registry. |
| CartesianFrame3D | Implemented | Finite resolved origin with right-handed orthonormal x/y/z unit axes expressed in one parent frame. | It cannot represent a reflected, scaled, or skewed basis. It does not itself identify a symbolic owner. |
| Global frame | Approved and implemented | Immutable resolved frame at `(0, 0, 0)` with `+X = (1,0,0)`, `+Y = (0,1,0)`, and vertical-up `+Z = (0,0,1)`. | It is not a project-specific analytical joint origin. |
| Member local frame | Approved construction; reusable builder implemented | Frame whose origin is `START`, local x follows `START` to `END`, and local z is constructed from an explicit projected reference with no fallback. | Stage 1.3C1 reuses this builder for separate geometry-layer member placement; the frame never reverses with connected end and no placement is stored in `AssemblyMember` or a `JointAssembly` registry. |
| Vector3D | Implemented | Finite geometric displacement or direction vector supporting standard Euclidean vector operations. | Public force, moment, and position types remain semantically distinct. |
| UnitVector3D | Implemented | `Vector3D` constrained to unit norm within the provisional dimensionless mathematical tolerance. | The tolerance is not a dimensional or engineering acceptance criterion. |
| Dimensionless mathematical tolerance | Provisional | Central value `1.0e-12` used only for unit-vector, orthogonality, proper-determinant, and angular near-parallel checks. | Requires future review before any dimensional fabrication, fit-up, hole-alignment, or engineering acceptance use. |
| RigidTransform3D | Implemented | Proper rotation followed by translation, with explicit composition order and inversion. | Translation affects points, not vectors; scaling, reflection, shear, and projective behavior are prohibited. |
| Authoritative component placement | Approved and implemented | Exactly one proper resolved placement for one member or connector in canonical global coordinates. | Stage 1.3C1 stores one `CartesianFrame3D` in the placed aggregate and derives any transform view; it stores no independent roll, mirror, parent hierarchy, analytical joint origin, or second placement authority. |
| PlacedComponentGeometry3D | Implemented; name provisional | Immutable Stage 1.3C1 aggregate associating one member or connector identity with its authoritative global frame, explicit longitudinal extent, explicit section-datum offset, exact Stage 1.3B cross-section, boundary planes, and analytic physical/deferred/void extrusions. | Supports no `AssemblySupport`; it is a framework-independent geometry contract, not a domain-entity mutation, `JointAssembly` registry, API DTO, persistence model, rendered solid, or calculation object. |
| Component reference line | Approved and implemented | Geometric line defined by the placed component frame's local `x` axis through local `(y, z) = (0, 0)`. | Placement reference only; it is not automatically a centroidal, shear-center, neutral, structural-analysis, joint, connection, or force line. |
| SectionDatumOffset | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Explicit finite immutable `offset_y` and `offset_z` locating the Stage 1.3B section construction datum relative to the component reference line in local `y-z`. | A section point maps to local `(x, y + offset_y, z + offset_z)`. Values are never inferred; zero means only datum-line/reference-line coincidence. |
| Section-datum line | Approved and implemented | Global image of local points `(x, offset_y, offset_z)` for a placed component. | Distinct from the component reference line unless both offsets are zero; coincidence conveys no centroidal, analytical, joint, or force-reference meaning. |
| LongitudinalExtent | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Exact finite local interval `[x_start, x_end]` with the strict dimensional requirement `x_end > x_start`. | No length tolerance, clamping, swapping, rounding, or automatic correction is allowed. A connector extent may cross zero; a member extent is exactly `[0, member length]`. |
| Physical longitudinal boundary plane | Approved and implemented; descriptor name provisional | Exact renderer-neutral minimum- or maximum-local-`x` plane carrying component/boundary identity, local `x`, reference-line point, section-datum point, and outward global unit normal. | Minimum outward normal is `-local x`; maximum is `+local x`. It is non-targetable geometry with no material direction, demand, resistance, interface, hole, bolt, or engineering status. |
| Member START plane | Approved and implemented | The member physical longitudinal boundary at minimum local `x`, equal to local `x = 0` for a member built from physical START and END. | Its outward normal is `-local x`; START identity remains fixed regardless of connected end. |
| Member END plane | Approved and implemented | The member physical longitudinal boundary at maximum local `x`, equal to local `x = member length`. | Its outward normal is `+local x`; END identity remains fixed regardless of connected end. |
| Connected-end plane | Approved and implemented | Pure deterministic selection of the member START or END physical plane from authoritative `AssemblyMember.connected_end`. | Selection does not reverse the frame or extent, change action signs, infer a joint point, or create connection targeting. |
| FrameInspection3D | Implemented | Renderer-neutral numerical origin, basis, norms, pairwise dot products, determinant, right-handedness, orthonormality, and geometric validity data. | Use `Valid frame` or `Invalid frame`; never engineering `PASS`, engineering `FAIL`, design status, or adequacy. |
| PointInFrame3D | Implemented | Existing finite position explicitly associated with one symbolic `CoordinateFrameReference`. | Used to enforce one identified common frame for explicit reference-point shifts; it is not an assembly placement registry. |
| ForceMomentSystem3D | Implemented | Semantically distinct force and moment reported at one `PointInFrame3D`. | Reference transfer is explicit and preserves force; it does not distribute demand or infer a point. |
| AxialLoadingSense | Approved and implemented | Controlled result `TENSION`, `COMPRESSION`, or `ZERO` derived from connected end and signed member-local `Fx`. | Interpretation never changes the stored sign or reverses the member-local frame. |
| ActionComponent | Approved and implemented | Exact component identity `FX`, `FY`, `FZ`, `MX`, `MY`, or `MZ`. | Linear components use positive frame axes; moment components use right-hand rotation about those axes. |
| PositiveActionDirection3D | Implemented | Permanent positive linear or rotational component direction expressed in parent/global coordinates. | Renderer-neutral; no signed entered magnitude or display styling. |
| AppliedActionDirection3D | Implemented | Component, linear/rotational kind, authoritative signed value, signed parent/global axis, sign/sense, and explicit zero status. | Renderer-neutral; contains no pixels, color, mesh, camera, tessellation, or display scale. |
| Component-local cross-section plane | Approved and implemented | The two-dimensional local `y-z` plane in which a member or connector's exact nominal cross-section is constructed. | Stage 1.3B supplies no extrusion length, component placement, or global transform; Stage 1.3C1 keeps that source immutable and extrudes it separately along placed component local `x`. |
| Section construction datum | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Deterministic point at the center of a standard section's nominal outside bounding box, represented as `(0, 0)` in the source local `y-z` plane. | Stage 1.3C1 may locate it with an explicit section offset. It is never inferred as the centroid, shear center, analytical line, joint point, force reference point, or proof that any of those coincide. |
| Nominal outside bounds | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Exact axis-aligned minimum and maximum local `y` and `z` coordinates of the complete nominal standard-section envelope. | Bounds define the construction datum and geometry envelope; they are not manufacturer tolerances or section properties. |
| Standard section dimensions | Approved meaning; implementation names provisional | Explicit finite positive inputs for supported W/I, channel, tee, rectangular/square tube, angle, plate/doubler, and round-tube nominal geometry. | They are user-supplied geometric values, not a built-in manufacturer catalog, canonical unit policy, fit-up tolerance, or property table. |
| SectionPoint2D | Implemented; name provisional | Finite local `y-z` coordinate used by exact nominal section geometry. | It has no local `x` coordinate and no global or assembly placement. |
| SectionBoundingBox2D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact axis-aligned outside bounds whose center is the section construction datum. | It is an envelope contract, not a centroidal or analytical property. |
| AxisAlignedRectangle2D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact positive-span rectangular primitive for flat-wall physical elements, finite deferred zones, and nominal voids. | Role and targetability come from its owning record, not from the primitive itself. |
| SectionLineSegment2D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact nonzero local `y-z` line used only to record a deferred nominal element-junction boundary. | It has no area, material ownership, targetability, fillet geometry, or structural credit. |
| Annulus2D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact analytic circular ring defined by center and inner and outer radii. | It is authoritative round-tube geometry; any future display tessellation is derived and cannot replace it. |
| PhysicalElementGeometry2D | Implemented; name provisional | Geometry associated one-to-one with an existing Stage 1.2 physical section-element identifier. | Its targetable flag means eligible physical geometry for a future targeting stage; Stage 1.3B creates no connection face, hole, interface assignment, or bolt penetration. |
| DeferredSectionFeature2D | Approved behavior and implemented; name provisional | Stable non-targetable geometric record for an angle heel, tube corner, or web/stem-flange meeting boundary whose engineering ownership or behavior is intentionally unsupported. | Distinct from a physical element, material region, nominal void, and `ConnectingComponentZone`; it creates no structural credit. |
| SectionVoid2D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact nominal rectangular opening or missing region stored separately from occupied and deferred geometry. | A void has no material ownership or targetability and is not a drilled connection hole. |
| CrossSectionGeometry2D | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Immutable canonical aggregate containing construction datum, outside bounds, section family, idealization, physical-element geometry, deferred features, nominal voids, and profile representation. | Covers supported standard nominal 2D sections only; excludes custom and solid-round geometry, section properties, global placement, faces, holes, targeting, calculations, API, persistence, and rendering. |
| LocalRectangularPrism3D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact local descriptor obtained by extruding one Stage 1.3B axis-aligned rectangle through the placed component's explicit local-`x` extent and applying its section offset in `y-z`. | Used within distinct physical-element, deferred-feature, or void wrappers as applicable; the primitive itself creates no Boolean solid, mesh, volume, material ownership, targetability, or structural credit. |
| LocalAnnularCylinder3D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact finite local annular-cylinder descriptor retaining `x_start`, `x_end`, shifted local `y-z` center, exact inner radius, and exact outer radius. | Authoritative round-tube extrusion remains analytic; no polygonization, tessellation, fixed circumferential material vector, volume, or section property is introduced. |
| LocalRuledSurface3D | Implemented; name provisional | Exact zero-thickness surface generated by extruding one deferred Stage 1.3B section line through the local-`x` extent. | Remains deferred and non-targetable, with no thickening, fillet, material ownership, structural credit, hole, interface, or resistance. |
| Placed physical element | Implemented; wrapper name provisional | Stable component/physical-element occurrence retaining exact local extrusion primitive(s), source Stage 1.3B targetability eligibility, and access to the one global placement frame. | Separate physical occurrences are never merged merely because Stage 1.2 maps them to one material region; no material-property data are copied or redefined. |
| Deferred extrusion | Approved behavior and implemented; wrapper name provisional | Exact local prism or zero-thickness ruled surface derived from one deferred Stage 1.3B rectangle or line. | `is_deferred` remains true and `is_targetable` remains false; no material ownership or structural credit is added. |
| Void extrusion | Implemented; wrapper name provisional | Exact local rectangular void-prism descriptor derived from one Stage 1.3B nominal rectangular void along the placed extent. | It remains nonmaterial, non-targetable, and not a physical element; no Boolean subtraction is performed. |
| Nominal sharp-corner idealization | Approved and implemented | Flat-wall standard-section representation that preserves the complete nominal footprint while keeping ambiguous corner and junction behavior explicit and unsupported. | It is not manufactured fillet/corner detail, a fabrication tolerance, or evidence of structural behavior. |
| Analytic round-tube geometry | Approved and implemented | Exact round-tube annulus based on outside diameter and wall thickness, centered on the section construction datum, with Stage 1.3C1 exact annular-cylinder placement along local `x`. | Cylindrical CW/TT evaluation fields, material properties, section properties, and calculation remain deferred; placement adds no fixed tangential material vector. |
| Surface patch | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Canonical bounded physical or support surface with stable participant-scoped identity, exact analytic geometry, source, exposure, disposition, and derived targetability. | Separate from its physical section element, material region, rendered mesh, camera-facing fragment, interface assignment, and calculation support. |
| SurfacePatchReference | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Stable pair of participant identifier and patch identifier that resolves within exactly one component surface set or identifies one bounded support surface. | It is a geometric reference only; Stage 1.3C2A does not store it on `ConnectionInterface` or establish attachment/load transfer. |
| ComponentSurfaceSet3D | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Immutable participant-scoped collection of deterministic surface patches derived from one exact placed standard component. | Patches are sorted by identifier and resolved only in their owning participant scope; it is not a `JointAssembly` registry or interface-target collection. |
| Surface exposure | Approved meaning and implemented; enum name provisional | Geometric classification distinguishing exterior-exposed, void-exposed, physical end-cut, and internal-junction patches. | Exposure is separate from deferred disposition, targetability, material direction, accessibility, contact, or structural qualification. |
| Surface disposition | Approved meaning and implemented; enum name provisional | Independent state distinguishing regular surface geometry from geometry derived from a deferred Stage 1.3B feature. | All deferred patches and all internal-junction patches are non-targetable; the two-dimensional state avoids a free targetability override. |
| Geometric targetability | Approved meaning and implemented | Derived eligibility of a regular non-internal surface patch to become a future connection-interface target. | It is not an attachment, load path, physical accessibility conclusion, material qualification, structural credit, calculation support, resistance, or result. |
| Planar surface frame | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Proper right-handed Cartesian frame whose origin and local `y-z` extents bound a planar rectangle or annulus and whose local `+x` is the signed plane normal. | The normal is geometric and does not define FRP LW/CW/TT material axes. Extents/radii remain exact canonical data rather than renderer bounds. |
| Analytic cylindrical surface | Approved meaning and implemented; name provisional | Exact finite cylinder retaining axis frame, positive extent, radius, and outward or inward radial normal sense. | It has no canonical angular origin, fixed tangent, polygon count, mesh identity, material property, or section property. |
| Bounded support surface | Approved meaning and implemented | Explicit support-owned planar rectangular patch defined by a proper frame, positive finite extents, stable identifier, and label. | It is not an infinite plane, inferred support body, thickness, contact model, restraint footprint, bearing area, structural credit, or calculation support. |
| Interface target | Approved and implemented; representation provisional | Geometry association between one logical `ConnectionInterface` participant and one or more bounded zones on exact participant-scoped planar surface patches. | Preserves participant order and source identities; establishes no contact, load distribution, attachment adequacy, or calculation support. |
| Connection zone | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Side-scoped stable selection of either one whole targetable planar patch or exact local rectangular bounds on a targetable planar rectangle. | Distinct from the authoritative surface, physical element, material region, rendered selection, hole, bolt pattern, contact region, and calculation-support state. |
| Interface target side | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Nonempty deterministic set of same-participant, same-signed-normal, coplanar resolved zones with exactly one primary zone. | May retain disconnected W/I flange/web or tube-wall end patches without merging them; primary means geometric reference only. |
| Primary zone | Approved and implemented; name provisional | One zone that supplies the primary target plane and source surface frame for the explicit first-side interface datum. | Does not mean governing demand, primary load path, contact priority, largest area, centroid, or bolt-group center. |
| Geometry comparison tolerance | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Explicit immutable strictly positive distance and dimensionless angular comparison values supplied by the caller. | A geometry aid only; not the Stage 1.3A mathematical tolerance, fabrication tolerance, fit criterion, unit-conversion policy, or engineering acceptance value. |
| Interface origin | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Explicit datum constructed from finite local `y-z` coordinates in the primary first-side surface frame. | May lie outside the zone/material footprint, including an annulus center; never inferred from centroid, shear center, bolt group, section datum, or joint point. |
| Interface-local frame | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Proper frame whose `+x` is the first-side signed normal, `+y` is an explicit reference projected into the plane, and `+z = +x cross +y`. | Participant order is not swapped, normals are not flipped, and no axis fallback is permitted. It carries no force or result. |
| Signed plane separation | Approved and implemented; name provisional | Raw signed distance between primary target planes measured along the first-side normal. | Coincidence within supplied tolerance is neutral geometry data, not contact, fit, interference, bearing, adequacy, or PASS/FAIL. |
| ConnectingComponentZone | Provisional legacy conceptual term | Named face, leg, stem, flange, web, or other region used to attach interfaces and organize future demand on a connecting component. | Do not confuse it with the implemented side-scoped geometric `Connection zone`; future demand organization remains unimplemented. |
| ReinforcementComponent | Approved concept; name provisional | Explicit component intended to alter or reinforce a host region, including a doubler. | Structural credit and attachment classification must be stored separately. |
| CalculationSnapshot | Approved concept; name provisional | Immutable record of canonical engineering input, versions, fingerprint, calculation results, warnings, assumptions, and applicability state at one calculation event. | Old snapshots remain historical; changed engineering input makes them stale for current use. |
| ReportSnapshot | Approved concept; name provisional | Immutable report artifact record linked to the exact `CalculationSnapshot` and presentation/version metadata from which it was generated. | Summary and Detailed reports use the same immutable result data. |
| CalculationFingerprint | Provisional | SHA-256 digest of canonically normalized engineering input and calculation-relevant versions. | Ownership metadata and display-only state are excluded. Hashing design is not yet approved. |
| Surface patch role | Approved meaning; name provisional | Explicit canonical physical role of a surface patch: opposing thickness face, edge, end cut, analytic tube face, junction, or support face. | Assigned by an authoritative factory; never inferred from names, rendering, dimensions, or material. |
| JointGeometryBasis | Approved meaning; name provisional | Complete exact pre-bolt in-memory geometry for one assembly: joint frame, placed components, surface sets, support surfaces, and resolved interfaces. | Required before bolt resolution; not a domain field, DTO, persistence record, or calculation input. |
| JointGeometryContext | Approved meaning; name provisional | `JointGeometryBasis` plus exactly one resolved geometry aggregate for every logical bolt group. | Resolves symbolic frames/reference points; creates no load path or calculation support. |
| Master bolt center | Approved concept | Sole canonical physical center derived from one logical bolt location in the resolved bolt-group frame. | Layer holes, views, dimensions, and future calculations must derive from it rather than duplicate or snapped centers. |
| Authoritative bolt axis | Approved concept | Infinite line through a master center in resolved bolt-group local `+x`. | Distinct from a finite layer hole cylinder and from display linework. |
| Intended penetrated layer | Approved concept; name provisional | Explicit participant physical element, negative-thickness entry face, positive-thickness exit face, connection-zone references, and round-hole diameter for one bolt path. | Does not imply drilling permission, bearing eligibility, or structural credit. |
| Resolved round hole | Approved concept; name provisional | Exact analytic cylinder centered on the authoritative bolt axis from one layer entry intersection to its exit intersection. | No Boolean subtraction, mesh, hardware, clearance acceptance, or resistance is implied. |
| Raw geometric clearance | Approved concept | Unclamped minimum distance from a resolved hole center to a patch or rectangular-zone boundary in that surface's local plane. | Compared with hole radius using an explicit geometric tolerance; not a fabrication or code criterion. |
| Raw interlayer gap | Approved concept | `following entry - preceding exit` along the authoritative bolt-axis parameter in declared layer order. | Retained even when slightly negative inside the explicit comparison band; never sorted or clamped. |
| Geometric stack span | Approved concept | Raw `final exit - first entry` axis-parameter distance across one declared bolt path. | Not a qualified grip length, hardware length, contact conclusion, or engineering criterion. |
| Bolt-group interface representation | Approved meaning; name provisional | One exact declared interface retained with raw signed normal alignment and signed plane offset relative to the primary bolt-group frame. | Additional normals must be parallel or antiparallel within the explicit angular comparison tolerance; this does not imply coincidence, shared demand, or force distribution. |
| Resolved reference point | Approved concept; name provisional | Original symbolic/explicit reference identity bound to one exact global physical position with frame and owner provenance. | Resolution does not approve template-permitted points or shift an action automatically. |
| Explicit eccentricity transform | Approved meaning; name provisional | Caller-directed action transfer from resolved P to resolved Q in an exact output frame using `(r_P - r_Q) cross F`, with complete immutable trace. | Not automatic eccentricity discovery, demand distribution, or a calculation result. |

| Calculation support | Approved concept | Capability state indicating that an approved, applicable, implemented, and verified calculation family can evaluate the condition. | Must fail closed when absent or unqualified. |
| Warning | Approved concept | Supplemental issue accompanying a result. | Cannot replace a required unsupported, incomplete, invalid, stale, or review-required state. |

| Physical quantity | Approved meaning; representation provisional | Exact finite Decimal magnitude paired with one controlled unit and physical dimension, convertible only within that dimension. | Canonical calculation units are mm, N, N-mm, MPa, and one; display choice is non-authoritative. |
| Published code unit basis | Approved concept | Explicit identity of the published U.S. customary or SI standard-hole rule used to generate a physical hole. | It is source provenance, not a display-unit preference. |
| Authoritative physical hole diameter | Approved concept | Stored result of nominal bolt diameter plus the exact increment from one published source basis. | It is not recomputed when converted or displayed in another unit. |

## Calculation Slice 8 terminology

**Independent in-plane free moment** is the signed `M_C,R` component of the
complete wrench, not a force-line offset. **Exact rational recovery** means
identity of numerator/denominator values for both forces and moments about the
centroid and original reference. **Decimal-80 projection** is a deterministic
interoperability representation, not an equilibrium tolerance. **Mechanics
compatibility** with Stage 2.5A preserves its native numerical path and compares
shared physical invariants, without demanding identical Decimal serialization.
`CALCULATED` denotes a demand result, not an engineering resistance PASS.

## Provisional naming boundary

Internally, `ConnectingComponent` is reserved for T-sections, plates, angles, gussets, brackets, and similar load-transferring parts. `Fastener` is the separate family for fastening hardware, and `Bolt` is a fastener specialization. This distinction prevents a directly bolted member interface from acquiring a fictitious connector and keeps component demand separate from bolt and bolt-stack demand. The naming is provisional; the physical distinctions and the requirement to model direct bolting explicitly are approved.

The controlled roadmap uses `clip-angle-type` for future angle connector assemblies.

Calculation Slice 7 RC2 distinguishes the **angle connector core** (material-
neutral geometry, references, complete wrenches, equilibrium and fingerprint)
from an **angle resistance provider** (material-specific applicability, sources
and resistance). **Qualified FRP full-wrench envelope** means exact geometry,
material, fastener, fixture, frame/reference and source-version binding with
separate positive/negative design strengths. It is not a universal FRP heel or
prying equation. **External support handoff** is a complete signed group wrench,
not individual anchor forces or a concrete/anchor-capacity result. A qualified
provider result is not an unqualified prescriptive PASS or full connection approval.
The owner-provided phrase “slip angle” remains terminology requiring confirmation only
if it is later intended to identify a distinct topology; Stage 3.1 does not create such
a distinction.

## Stage 4.2 component, source and status terminology

A component wrench includes all three force and three moment components at its stated reference. Half web wrench means one-half of the complete web wrench after exact symmetry proof, shifted separately to each physical interface. Heel-local web coordinates retain B=1.25/2.75 and reference B=2; they are not recentered. Native governing failure is the unchanged group-mode selection, not a UI-selected bearing failure. Missing qualified sources are visible but cannot hide evaluated FAIL. External wall handoff is four group wrenches and one assembled wall wrench, never individual anchor forces or concrete/anchor capacity.
